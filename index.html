Privacy Policy for ZorroMail

Last updated: 21 July 2026

ZorroMail (the "App") is a mobile email client that lets business mailbox users read and send email from a mailbox provisioned by their administrator. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used. We designed ZorroMail to collect as little personal information as possible.

Who this policy applies to

ZorroMail is used with a mailbox account (email address and password) issued by your organization's ZorroMail administrator. Your organization is the controller of your mailbox data; the App is the tool you use to access it.

Information the App handles

  • Account credentials. When you sign in, your email address and password are sent to the ZorroMail server to authenticate you. The App does not store your password. Session tokens returned by the server are stored securely on your device using the operating system's encrypted keystore (Apple Keychain via expo-secure-store).
  • Mailbox content. Emails, folders, contacts, attachments, signatures, and mailbox settings are retrieved from and sent to the ZorroMail server so the App can display and manage your inbox. This content is processed to provide the service and is not used for advertising.
  • Photos and files you choose. If you attach a photo or file, or set a profile or contact photo, the App accesses only the specific item you select from your photo library or files. It does not scan or upload your library. Selected items are sent to the ZorroMail server as part of the email or setting you are creating.

How your information is used

Information is used solely to provide the App's core functionality: authenticating you, displaying and organizing your mailbox, and sending the messages and settings you create. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking across apps or websites.

Where your data goes

The App communicates only with the ZorroMail backend service over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to deliver mail functionality. Your mailbox data is stored on that service, which is operated by your organization / the ZorroMail service provider. No mailbox data is shared with any other third party by the App.

Data retention

Session tokens remain on your device until you sign out, at which point they are removed. Mailbox content is retained on the ZorroMail server according to your organization's mailbox policy. To delete your mailbox or account, contact your ZorroMail administrator.

Security

Authentication tokens are kept in the device's secure, encrypted storage, and all communication with the server uses HTTPS. No security measure is perfect, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children's privacy

ZorroMail is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at support@zorromail.com.
